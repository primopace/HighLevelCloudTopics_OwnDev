What key rotation ACTUALLY means

When IdentityServer rotates keys, it does this:

Before rotation:
Key A
 ├── PrivateA (signing)
 └── PublicA  (JWKS)

After rotation:
Key A (still valid)
 ├── PrivateA
 └── PublicA

Key B (new)
 ├── PrivateB (used for new tokens)
 └── PublicB


👉 Multiple private keys live at the same time
